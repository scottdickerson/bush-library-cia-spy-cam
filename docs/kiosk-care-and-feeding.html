What normal looks like

It runs itself

Two screens

The large screen shows the CIA seal and "Place hand on scanner below". The small touchscreen below it shows "Place right hand".

Always on

The exhibit stays on all the time. After a restart or a power outage it turns itself back on and shows both screens within a couple of minutes. You don't need to press or type anything.

A visitor touches the scanner

The touchscreen plays the hand scan, and the large screen plays a "satellite" sequence of about 75 seconds.

The live feed

About 40 seconds in, the box marked "Live feed from thermal X-ray sensor" shows the visitor in heat-map colors for about 20 seconds. That's a real camera aimed at the visitor.

After the sequence

Both screens go back to the seal and "Place right hand" on their own. Touching the scanner again during a sequence does nothing until it's finished.

No sound

The exhibit is silent. That's normal.

Around 3 AM

The computer restarts itself every night to install Windows updates. The screens may be dark for a few minutes, then the seal comes back.

Before you fix anything

First, wait

If a sequence is playing, let it finish. It takes about a minute and a quarter, then both screens go back to the start by themselves. Most "stuck" screens are a sequence that's still playing.

The one fix that solves most things

Restart the computer

Use whichever of these you can.

With the keyboard
  1. Plug in the keyboard.
  2. Press Ctrl + Alt + Delete together.
  3. Tap the power icon in the bottom-right corner, then Restart.
  4. Wait. Both screens come back by themselves in a couple of minutes.
  5. Unplug the keyboard and put it away.

Don't press Esc while the exhibit is running: it closes the exhibit. If that happens, restart the computer.

Without a keyboard
  1. Press and hold the computer's power button for about 10 seconds, until it turns off.
  2. Wait 10 seconds.
  3. Press the power button once. Both screens come back by themselves in a couple of minutes.

This is fine to do now and then. Don't make a habit of it if the keyboard is available.

Common problems

What do you see?

The live feed box stays black or frozen

  • The camera connects through a long network cable with a small box at each end (a USB extender). Check that both boxes have their lights on and that their cables are plugged in firmly.
  • Check that nothing is covering or blocking the camera.
  • Restart the computer. The rest of the exhibit still works without the camera; only the live feed box stays black.

The live feed shows the wrong spot

  • The camera has been bumped. Gently aim it back at where visitors stand at the scanner.

Touching the scanner does nothing

  • Wait for any sequence on the large screen to finish, then touch once.
  • If the touchscreen is dark, check its power light and its power adapter.
  • If it ignores every touch, check that its cables are plugged in firmly at both ends, then restart the computer.

A screen is black

  • Between about 3 and 3:15 AM this is normal. It's installing updates.
  • Check the monitor's power light. If it's off, press the monitor's power button.
  • Check the computer's power light. If it's off, press the computer's power button once.
  • If everything's on and it's still black after 5 minutes, restart the computer.

The taskbar appears at the bottom of a screen

  • Someone swiped up from the bottom edge. Tap the middle of the exhibit and it goes away.
  • If it stays, restart the computer.

A message box appears over the exhibit

  • Take a photo of it with your phone.
  • If it has a Close, OK, or X button, tap that. Don't tap anything else in it.
  • If it comes back, restart the computer, then send the photo to the person on the contact line above.
Call for help

The screens are swapped, or you see the Windows desktop, a password screen, or "We weren't able to start your app"

  • Restart the computer once.
  • If it comes back the same way, don't try to sign in or change anything. Take a photo and contact the person on the contact line above.

For the technical contact

  • Setup, the kiosk scripts, and Windows troubleshooting are in the README at github.com/scottdickerson/bush-library-cia-spy-cam. Kiosk options are compared in starting-electron-app-automatically.md.
  • To reach the admin account, press Ctrl + Alt + Delete and sign out. The app is installed in C:\Program Files\CIA Spycam\.
  • Windows runs the kiosk in Assigned Access restricted mode, which doesn't relaunch the app if it quits; a restart brings it back.
  • The touch window goes on the display Windows reports as touch-capable. If the screens are swapped, redo Tablet PC Settings > Setup > Touch input (README: "The windows are on the wrong displays").

Admin sign-in

Only use this if the technical contact asks you to, for example over the phone. Visitors must never see this page: anyone with this password can turn off the kiosk's protections.

Username Password
  1. Plug in the keyboard and press Ctrl + Alt + Delete, then choose Sign out.
  2. On the sign-in screen, choose the admin account (or Other user and type the username), then type the password.
  3. When you're done, restart the computer. It goes back to the exhibit by itself.
