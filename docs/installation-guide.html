Bush Library · CIA Spy Cam exhibit

Installation guide

How to connect the computer, both screens, and the camera. The two screens are wired the same way as the original exhibit; the camera now connects over USB through an extender.

The big picture

What connects to what

Computer ──── video ──────────────► Main monitor (seal screen)
Computer ──── video ──────────────► Touchscreen (hand scan)
Computer ◄─── touch USB ─────────── Touchscreen
Computer ──── USB ────────────────► Extender, computer end
Extender, computer end ═══ network cable (existing run) ═══ Extender, camera end
Power supply ─────────────────────► Extender, camera end
Extender, camera end ── USB ──────► USB camera (in the satellite)

Plus power to the computer, both monitors, and the camera-end extender.

Before you start

Parts

Computer

Minisforum mini PC, with its power adapter.

Main monitor

The large screen that shows the CIA seal. Video cable and power cord.

Touchscreen

The small hand-scan screen. Video cable, touch USB cable, and power adapter.

USB extender

StarTech pair: one box for the computer end and one for the camera end, plus the camera end's power supply.

Network cable

The existing cable run from the computer to the satellite.

USB camera

Logitech USB webcam, mounted in the satellite.

Power strip

For the computer, both monitors, and the extender's power supply.

Step by step

Wiring it up

1

Place the computer

Set the mini PC where it can reach both monitors and the extender. Don't turn it on yet.

2

Connect the touchscreen

Same three connections as the original exhibit:

  • Video from the computer to the touchscreen.
  • Touch USB from the touchscreen to a USB port on the computer.
  • Power to the touchscreen's power adapter.
3

Check the touchscreen's position

The touchscreen sits below the main monitor, upright (portrait), where visitors place their hand.

4

Connect the main monitor

Just two connections:

  • Video from the computer to the main monitor.
  • Power to the main monitor.
5

USB from the computer to the extender

Plug a normal USB cable into a USB port on the computer.

6

Computer end of the extender

Plug the other end of that USB cable into the extender's computer-end box. Plug the network cable into the box's network port.

This box gets its power over USB from the computer; it doesn't need a power supply.

7

Run the network cable to the satellite

Use the existing network cable run to the satellite. Plug it into the network port on the extender's camera-end box.

8

Power the camera end

The camera-end box needs its own power. Plug its power supply into the box and into the power strip. Its lights come on when it's powered and linked.

9

Plug in the camera

Plug the USB camera in the satellite into the USB port on the camera-end box. Aim it at where visitors stand at the hand scanner.

Last step

Turn it on and test

  1. Turn on both monitors, then the computer.
  2. Within a couple of minutes the main monitor shows the CIA seal and "Place hand on scanner below", and the touchscreen shows "Place right hand".
  3. Touch the hand on the touchscreen. The hand scan plays and the main monitor starts its sequence.
  4. About 40 seconds in, the "Live feed" box on the main monitor shows the camera image in heat-map colors. Check that it shows the person at the scanner.
  5. After about 75 seconds both screens go back to the start by themselves.

If something's wrong

  • The live feed box stays solid blue or black: check both extender boxes have their lights on, the camera end has power, and the network cable is plugged in firmly at both ends. Then restart the computer.
  • The screens are swapped (the hand shows on the big screen): the computer needs to be told which screen is the touchscreen. See "The windows are on the wrong displays" in the README.
  • Touching does nothing: check the touchscreen's touch USB cable is plugged into the computer.
